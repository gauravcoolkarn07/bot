About Us

A Legacy of Academic Excellence

DY Patil College of Engineering and Technology, Kolhapur, stands as one of Maharashtra's most respected technical institutions. Founded under the visionary leadership of Dr. D.Y. Patil, the college has been shaping engineering minds for over four decades.

Our state-of-the-art campus spans across 25 acres, equipped with world-class laboratories, research centers, and a rich library. We believe in holistic education that extends beyond classrooms — preparing students not just as engineers, but as thought leaders.

Begin Your Engineering Journey

Register Online Fill the online application form with your academic details, contact information, and preferred course.
02
Document Submission Upload scanned copies of SSC, HSC/Diploma marksheets, Aadhar, caste certificate (if applicable), and passport photo.

Entrance / Merit Screening Admission is based on MHT-CET score or 12th percentile through CAP rounds conducted by the state government.
